# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-25)

The first release, as apps for macOS and Windows.

- Your playlists are the game. Add one from Spotify (a CSV file from Exportify), from a Deezer link, or build it here by searching for songs, then press Play on its cover.
- Hear a tenth of a second, guess, and get a longer clip after every miss: 0.1, 0.5, 2, 8 and 15 seconds, five tries.
- Endless rounds with no repeats until every song has played, or one song of the day per playlist.
- Mixes put several playlists in one game, each with its own tab.
- Stats with streaks and how many tries each win took, and a result to share.
- Clips come from Deezer's 30-second previews, so they often start partway into a song.
