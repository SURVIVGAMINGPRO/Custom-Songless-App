# Changelog

## 1.1.1 (2026-10-26)

- The play symbol sits in the middle of its button now.
- Keyboard shortcuts, which you can change in Settings → Keyboard Shortcuts:
  - **Enter** guesses the song you've picked (or the top suggestion), or skips when the search is empty. After a round, Enter goes to the next song.
  - **Shift-Enter** skips even with something typed.
  - **Space** plays or stops the clip (and still types once the search has text).
  - **/** jumps to the search, **S** shares your result, **M** switches between Endless and Today's Song, **?** shows How to Play.
- Clicking Play, Skip or a mode no longer takes the keyboard away from the search, so you can keep typing.

## 1.1.0 (2026-10-26)

- A new look in the style of Apple's apps: a sidebar with your playlists and mixes, large titles, grouped lists, and the system font. On the Mac the sidebar runs up to the window buttons, like Music.
- Light and dark mode, following your system. Settings → Appearance can pick one.
- Guessing only shows songs from the playlist you're playing, as you type. No more covers, karaoke versions and other songs from all of Deezer.
- Guess rows show an icon for each result (tick, artist, cross), so colour isn't the only signal.
- Each playlist has its own page with its artwork, a song list, and Play, Rename and Delete.
- When you finish a round, the cover grows while the preview plays, as in Apple Music.

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-25)

The first release, as apps for macOS and Windows.

- Your playlists are the game. Add one from Spotify (a CSV file from Exportify), from a Deezer link, or build it here by searching for songs, then press Play on its cover.
- Hear a tenth of a second, guess, and get a longer clip after every miss: 0.1, 0.5, 2, 8 and 15 seconds, five tries.
- Endless rounds with no repeats until every song has played, or one song of the day per playlist.
- Mixes put several playlists in one game, each with its own tab.
- Stats with streaks and how many tries each win took, and a result to share.
- Clips come from Deezer's 30-second previews, so they often start partway into a song.
